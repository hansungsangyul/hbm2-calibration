# A12 — Bidirectional single-stage interventions on the cross-pipeline difference between model A and a Python implementation of the reference pipeline: pre-registration

Registered after the A8 cross-pipeline results (Table 8 of the corrected manuscript) and the specification of R (Section III-B, S9) were known, but before any A12 intervention image or result was inspected. The commit timestamp records the freeze of this protocol; subsequent changes are logged as amendments. Addresses Limitation 2, Section VIII item (ii) and Reviewer 3 Concern 9 of Access-2026-52990: which individual stage substitutions change false alarms and defect recognition when applied to either the model-A background or the Python reference-pipeline background, and whether any substitution brings the evaluated metrics close to their model-A values. These interventions estimate background-specific effects; they do not provide an additive decomposition of the complete cross-pipeline difference or identify all interactions. Everything is done at the data level with the existing checkpoints; no retraining. Design reviewed by Codex (thread 01a14805, 2026-10-17) before execution; its requested changes are incorporated here.

## 1. Content and labels
S consists of 500 images spanning the full held-out index range of the content C (download/HBM_clean): n_k = 251 + floor(1750k/500), k = 0, …, 499 (0251, 0254, 0258, …, 1997). The index list is frozen before variant generation and shared by every configuration and contrast. Labels download/HBM_labels (tile level, 5×20 grid). The same border/crop (320×512), tile grid, masks and per-image metric code as A8 (a8_paired_eval.py / a7_labels_eval2.py). All 20 archived terminal checkpoints (final.pth) per configuration are included, irrespective of training stability. Image and run distributions are reported separately; tiles and repeated checkpoint evaluations are not treated as independent specimens.

## 2. Operators and the intervention graph
The intervention graph has the ordered slots pre → noise → blur → vignette → post-noise → contrast → ring → JPEG → resampling. Model A uses its archived operators in the first seven slots (training-time prenorm in "pre" for AB-1, D_calib, D_sigma_p and D_v5e; identity otherwise; σ_post > 0 only for D_sigma_p and D_v5e) and identity operations for JPEG and resampling. The Python reference pipeline (R-port) uses identity operations for pre, post-noise and ring, and the operators below in the remaining slots. Post-noise and ring are separate intervention factors and are never bundled with contrast. The blur factor includes both the kernel and the boundary condition; the noise factor includes all clipping operations assigned to that block; the JPEG factor includes its input quantization, encoding and decoding; the resampling factor includes the uint8 handling of the source script.

R-port (Python, numpy/cv2/PIL; a12_rport.py) re-implements only_add_noise.m, level 2, local function add_realistic_noise_aggressive (HBM_paper/matlab/only_add_noise.m, saved 2025-10-09 12:53), in the script's order, from the source code (operator manifest):
- r1 noise block: `imnoise(x,'gaussian',0,1.5g)` (additive N(0, var 1.5g), output clipped to [0,1] by imnoise); `imnoise(.,'salt & pepper',d)` (uniform u: pepper for u < d/2, salt for d/2 ≤ u < d); `+ randn·0.6g` followed by the script's explicit `max(0,min(1,·))`. The script's first call, `imnoise(x,'poisson')` on double input, is numerically negligible (S9) and omitted.
- r2 motion blur: `fspecial('motion', L, φ)` (anti-aliased line kernel; ported from the MATLAB algorithm, unit cases checked: L = 5, φ = 0 → 1×5 uniform; φ = 90 → 5×1), `imfilter(.,PSF,'conv','circular')` → true convolution with circular boundary.
- r3 vignette: 1 − s·d²/d²max with MATLAB's 1-based meshgrid and centre (cols/2, rows/2), floored at m.
- r4 imadjust: linear map [il, ih] → [ol, oh] with clipping of the input range (gamma 1).
- r5 JPEG: `imwrite(double,'Quality',q)` → im2uint8 (round(255x)) → JPEG → `imread` (uint8). Implemented with OpenCV's libjpeg encoder/decoder at the same quality setting; equal quality settings do not establish equivalent decoded images, so this is an approximation whose version is recorded.
- r6 resampling: the script's explicit-size calls `imresize(u8, round(size·c))` then `imresize(., size)` (bicubic, anti-aliased on shrinking, uint8 in/out). Implemented with PIL BICUBIC (Keys a = −0.5, support scaled on shrinking, uint8 in/out) as the closest available implementation; coordinate-convention and kernel differences are an approximation, listed here before evaluation and limiting the interpretation of the resampling contrast.
The output of the full R-port is uint8-quantized (as R is stored as PNG). Variants in the A background that end with identity JPEG/resampling are not quantized (as in A8); the 8-bit quantization is listed as a residual difference between the two backgrounds.

Per-image parameters: for each image, the complete reference-parameter vector is drawn once with numpy RandomState(800000 + n), in the script's order (g ~ N(0.04, 0.012) ≥ 0; d ~ N(0.015, 0.005) in [0, 0.05]; L = max(1, round N(6, 2)); φ ~ N(45°, 30°); s ~ N(0.6, 0.2) in [0,1]; m ~ N(0.6, 0.1) in [0.3, 1]; il ~ N(0.1, 0.03) in [0, 0.3]; ih ~ N(0.9, 0.03) in [0.7, 1]; ol ~ N(0.2, 0.05) in [0, 0.4]; oh ~ N(0.8, 0.05) in [0.6, 1]; q = round N(60, 10) in [40, 95]; c ~ N(1.04, 0.015) in [0.98, 1.08]) and stored (a12_params.json). Random fields: the R noise block draws its three fields from a separate stream RandomState(810000 + n) consumed only inside that block; model-A fields ε1, ε2, ε3, ε_post come from torch.manual_seed(900000 + n) in the fixed order of a8_paired_gen.py and are generated whether or not a stage is used. Omitting or replacing a stage therefore does not alter any untouched stage's parameter draw or random field; shared fields are reused across configurations and variants wherever the corresponding operation is retained. The "fixed" setting replaces the sampled parameter vector by the declared nominal central values (g 0.04, d 0.015, L 6, φ 45°, s 0.6, m 0.6, il 0.1, ih 0.9, ol 0.2, oh 0.8, q 60, c 1.04) while retaining the same noise fields.

## 3. Variants (per configuration; same per-image draws everywhere)
- V_A: A side = existing a8_paired outputs (metrics from a8_paired_per_image.csv restricted to S).
- V_R: actual R = download/HBM_dirty2 (metrics from a7_labels_v2_per_image.csv, set R_heldout, declared mode, restricted to S).
- V_P: the Python reference pipeline with the sampled parameter vectors. V_F: the Python reference pipeline at the nominal central parameter values with the same noise realizations; the V_P–V_F contrast measures the effect of replacing the sampled parameter vectors by this fixed vector and does not isolate parameter variance independently of nonlinear transformations or interactions.
- Forward substitution I_k: start from model A and replace only slot k by the corresponding reference-pipeline operation, including identity where that operation is absent from R (I_post: σ_post removed; I_ring: ring removed; I_pre: prenorm removed).
- Reverse substitution L_k: start from the Python reference pipeline and replace only slot k by the configuration's model-A operation, including identity where that operation is absent from A (L_jpeg, L_res: stage omitted; L_post, L_ring, L_pre: the A operation inserted).
- Factors: noise, blur, vignette, post-noise, contrast, ring, JPEG, resampling, pre. A factor whose two operations are identical for a configuration (post-noise where σ_post = 0; pre where the configuration trained without prenorm) is an identity contrast and is not evaluated.
All untouched slots retain the parent pipeline's operations and random fields. The active variant manifest (a12_manifest.json) is frozen before evaluation. Closure checks before the run: the all-A setting reproduces the stored A8 output (V_A) and the all-reference setting reproduces V_P under the declared numerical tolerance (max |diff| ≤ 1e-5 and 0, respectively); these are implementation checks, not intervention outcomes. V_P, V_F, L_jpeg, L_res (and L_pre, shared by the prenorm configurations) are generated once and shared across configurations.

## 4. Evaluation
All 8 configurations × 20 runs, inference preprocessing identity (per-image z-score for the z-score configuration), batch inference as in A8 (fp32, batch 32). Per-image metrics: normal-tile pixel FPR; normal-tile FAR, defect-tile recall and class-correct recall at τ = 0.5 (primary; τ = 0.25, 0.75 stored). For a variant V, F(V) is the median over runs of the per-run median over S of the metric; IQR over runs is reported with it. Paired per-image differences I_k − V_A and L_k − V_P (per-run median → median/IQR over runs) are reported separately and are not substituted into the aggregate-share formula. Descriptive only; no hypothesis tests.

## 5. Behavioural agreement of the port (decided before results)
For each configuration, agreement between V_P and V_R is assessed with normal-tile FAR, normal-tile pixel FPR and class-correct defect-tile recall at τ = 0.5: for each metric and run, the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles over S are computed, then the median over runs of each percentile; the gate requires an absolute V_P–V_R difference ≤ 0.10 for every listed summary. Defect-tile recall and the secondary thresholds are reported but do not establish agreement by themselves. The tolerance is an operational screening criterion, not a statistical equivalence margin or proof of operator fidelity. For a configuration that fails, the interventions are interpreted only as interventions on the Python pipeline; for one that passes, behavioural correspondence with R may be discussed, while stage attribution remains conditional on the implementation. V_P is not tuned after detector outcomes are inspected. Secondary diagnostics (reported, not criteria): image mean and std, flat-patch std, 8-pixel blockiness, gradient-tensor coherence and orientation for V_R, V_P, V_F on the first 200 images of S (a12_fidelity_stats.json). Visual check: the first four images of S (0251, 0254, 0258, 0261) as {clean, V_A, V_R, V_P, each variant for D_manual} are inspected for implementation errors only.

## 6. Interpretation rules (fixed in advance)
G = F(V_P) − F(V_A) in normal-tile FAR. Forward share s_k = (F(I_k) − F(V_A))/G; reverse share r_k = (F(V_P) − F(L_k))/G; parameter-vector share (F(V_P) − F(V_F))/G. Shares are reported only when G > 0.10; otherwise absolute contrasts are reported and the share is marked undefined. A forward substitution with s_k ≥ 0.5 is described as producing at least half of the aggregate port-to-A FAR difference in the A background; a reverse substitution with r_k ≥ 0.5 as removing at least half of that difference in the port background. These are descriptive thresholds, not tests of necessity or sufficiency; shares may be negative, exceed one or sum to more than one and are not additive allocations; interactions are not estimated; all conclusions are conditional on the two tested backgrounds. A reduction in FAR is described as false-alarm reduction. An A-like operating outcome is declared only if the variant's F(FAR) is within 0.05 of V_A and its F(class-correct recall) is no more than 0.05 below V_A (same aggregation, τ = 0.5); these predeclared tolerances do not establish equivalence. All outcomes concern modified synthetic inputs evaluated with fixed detectors; no claim about measured CT data; no claim that the mechanism of the failure on the actual R has been identified. Results enter Section VI-E, Limitation 2, Section VIII (ii) and the response to Reviewer 3 Concern 9; the supplementary material receives the full tables.
