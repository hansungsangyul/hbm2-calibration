# A12 — Amendment 1 (am1): joint noise-and-blur substitutions

Registered 2026-10-17 19:55 UTC, after inspection of the completed single-block results of the main manifest (115 of 141 variant–configuration pairs complete at that time: V_P, V_F, all reverse substitutions L_*, and the forward substitutions I_noise, I_blur, I_vig; a12_per_image.csv and a12_summary.json as of that time), and before any joint variant was generated or evaluated. The remaining single-block forward substitutions (I_post, I_con, I_ring, I_jpeg, I_res, I_pre) of the main manifest may complete, and be inspected, before am1 is run; am1 is run only after the main manifest has finished (single writer to the per-image CSV). Design reviewed by Codex (thread 01a14805) before registration. The original pre-registration (A12_FACTORIAL_PREREG.md, commit 8c2fe60) is unchanged.

## Motivation (outcome-informed)
Amendment am1 adds joint noise-and-blur substitutions after inspection of the completed single-block results, which showed large forward FAR increases for both blocks (I_noise: FAR 1.000 in every configuration; I_blur: FAR 0.900–1.000) and no A-like outcome among the completed reverse substitutions (L_noise: FAR 0.298–1.000 by configuration; L_blur: 0.694–1.000; all other reverse substitutions 0.966–1.000). The amendment is frozen before either joint variant is generated or evaluated. These are outcome-informed follow-up analyses, distinguished from the originally pre-registered single-block interventions.

## Design and stopping rule
Evaluate L_noise_blur (the Python reference pipeline with both the noise block and the blur block replaced by the configuration's model-A operations) and I_noise_blur (model A with both the reference noise block and the reference blur block) for all eight configurations using the same S, all 20 checkpoints, preprocessing, metrics, aggregation and A-like criterion as the original protocol (a12_rport.amendment1_specs; `a12_run.py am1`). All untouched operations and random fields are retained, and the joint variants are compared with their parent pipeline and with both corresponding single-block variants. All joint outcomes are reported, including failures and FAR–recall trade-offs; no additional combinations are included in am1.

## Interpretation rule
The primary question is whether jointly replacing noise and blur in the Python reference background meets the existing A-like criterion (F(FAR) within 0.05 of V_A and F(class-correct recall) no more than 0.05 below V_A), and how its FAR and class-correct recall differ from the two single-block substitutions. A positive result supports a joint input-level intervention within this implementation; a negative result leaves the remaining pipeline differences and interactions unresolved. Because FAR is bounded and several forward conditions are saturated, non-additivity on this scale is not by itself interpreted as a mechanistic interaction. The blur block includes the kernel and the boundary handling, and the noise block includes its intensity, clipping and component specification; effects are attributed to blocks, never to a single attribute such as directionality.
